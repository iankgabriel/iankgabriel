# Hi, I'm Ian 👋

**AI Automation Specialist at IKG Digital.** I automate the repetitive work that slows small businesses down.

I build workflows in Make, n8n, Zapier, and GoHighLevel, and connect them to AI models, CRMs, spreadsheets, and team chat. Every build gets tested on the cases that usually break things, like an invoice with no matching record or a call that comes in after hours, before it goes live.

## What I build

- **AI inbox triage** that labels incoming messages, drafts replies, and routes them to the right Slack channel
- **Invoice and document checks** that match each invoice to its purchase order and flag mismatches right away
- **AI agents grounded in a knowledge base**, so they answer from real documents and admit when they don't know
- **CRM follow-up** for GoHighLevel: instant lead replies, missed-call text-back, and pipeline syncing to Google Sheets
- **Scheduled reports** that pull data from several sources and post a summary without anyone asking
